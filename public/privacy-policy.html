Privacy Policy

Bee Here With Me · Effective September 2, 2026

Private by design

Bee Here With Me does not require an account or a profile. You do not need to provide a name, diagnosis, birthday, photograph, medical history, family information, or other personal details. Each visit begins fresh.

Information we collect

Bee Here With Me does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data. The app does not include advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking.

Device permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, files, or notifications.

Data retention and deletion

Because Bee Here With Me does not collect or retain personal data, there is no personal data stored by us to delete. Closing or restarting the app begins a new visit.

Children's privacy

Bee Here With Me is intended as a general support tool for caregivers and visitors. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

General support only

Bee Here With Me provides general educational and communication support and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Changes to this policy

If the app's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes are released. The effective date above shows when this policy was last updated.
